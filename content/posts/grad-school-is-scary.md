That’s what I thought until I became a part of the atmosphere 2 weeks ago. The past few weeks have been stupidly good to me. So much so that I feel like I’ve grown as an individual more in the last couple days than I did in the last few years of my life.

I guess the feeling of me going to CMU and what it means never really sunk in until our orientation last week, when the professors introduced the courses and the tracks that our course endows us with the opportunity to take. I’ve heard so much about how prestigious CMU is and how wonderful the alumni are, but I’m guessing it’s my impostor syndrome that never really lets it stick to me. *“Surely it can't be that good… I wonder why they chose me.”* The amount of interactions I’ve had with amazing people at the forefront of what I want to do, the conversations I've had surrounding their work (and the secret linkedin stalking sessions that i undertook later) and the passion emanating from them is infectious enough for me to realise the how truth the first point is. This place IS different. For the latter… hmm I guess i have 16 months to figure that out!

It’s been 3 days since classes started and I got to meet everyone in my cohort. The classes themselves weren’t that hefty the first 2 days, but things reallyyyy kicked off today.\
Monday was spent attending lectures, trying to squeeze in some leetcode in the library and following up on RA apps I mailed last week. While doing so, I got a chance to talk to a guy facing the same conundrum as me - whether to drop DL systems or LLM systems. A lot of claude-ing the pros and cons and how it aligns with my interview prep and side work pushed me towards dropping the latter, but man oh man the lecture itself pulled me back in. Exactly the things I wanted to learn and taught in such a DIY way. I need to do scrappy work. I need to do projects by myself and learn from my mistakes. I need to re-learn the art of coding and the art of thinking. This course would help me do exactly that. Learning CUDA seems daunting but I think I can handle it. That coupled with creating your own project and implementing a paper on systems? I’M SO EXCITEDDD. Anyway, during that course I met a really cool guy who interned at Mistral and is gonna work full time. Totally not gonna hit him up and beg for tips. You see how cool people I'm meeting?

Anyway, coming on to yesterday, beautiful beautiful day. DL systems is legendary bro wdym the founders of XGBoost and qLoRA are teaching this course?? Absolutely insane. Anyway, even though the day was long, I found myself being really productive after class. I came back home and surprisingly stayed glued to the chair until midnight, without a speck of tiredness infecting my system. I did some data extraction for a small paper, stalked some amazing TAs and subsequently discovered another lab ***I neeeeed*** to work with, mailed the phd student I liked - all in a few hours' work. Need to stick with this pace fr.

Today was legendary. The GenAI lecture made me feel like I doubled my neurons and cognitive capacity within the span of an hour. I was GLUED to every word that Aran was saying. He speedran the attention mechanism and the basics of transformers but?? I think I lowkey loved it?? All the lectures I’ve seen and the videos I used to help me understand this concept helped lay the base, but I think today is when I really learned and imbued it into my brain. I also got a chance to talk to the LLM systems prof and the TA and the other dude who was confused to help me understand what to drop, but drumroll… I’m gonna drop this one but still attend all the lectures. They are not gonna miss me with all the learning that I’m about to load on my encephalon.

That brings me to now. My first blog entry I suppose. Hopefully there will be more to come. But man oh man, I’m gonna show this impostor syndrome who’s the boss. I’m meeting such cool people, it’s time to learn from the best and become one too. The energy is too infectious to not surrender!!
